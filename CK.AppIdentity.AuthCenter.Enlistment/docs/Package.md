The part of the Authentication Center in the enlistment of a CK-AppIdentity party. `AuthCenterApprovalCountersignature`
countersigns the statement of an approval (after checking the operator's live proof) and signs the `PinGrant` of a "both
approval"; the holder of the pending approval requires the countersignature. `AuthCenterPeeringAuthority` is the
`IPeeringAuthority` of the System: it states the approval page (`EnlistmentUrlStatement`), so that a waiting party that
anchors the Authentication Center receives an authenticated enlistment URL.
