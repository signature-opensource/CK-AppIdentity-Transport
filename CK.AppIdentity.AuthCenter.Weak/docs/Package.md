The weak logins of the CK Authentication Center: the "Weak" option of the Authentication Center, the "Weak" (a weak actor, at most at the Unsafe level) scheme family.

It holds the family only (the scheme, no account creation): the login provider is the backend's, referenced apart.
`"AuthCenter": { "Weak": false }` turns the scheme off for a party.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-AppIdentity-Transport/blob/stable/CK.AppIdentity.AuthCenter.Weak/README.md).
