The commands of the run-time tenant domains of a CK-AppIdentity System: `IGetTenantHostsQCommand` (the applications that
hold tenant templates, on the host of the operator's page), and the party commands sent to such an application:
`IGetTenantInstancesQCommand`, `ICreateTenantInstanceCommand`, `ISetTenantIndexCommand`, `IRemoveTenantInstanceCommand`.
They are Critical. The handlers are in CK.AppIdentity.Tenants.
