The commands between the parties of a CK-AppIdentity System and their Authentication Center: the directory of the
encryption keys, the revocation of party identities and the verification of the users' proofs relayed by other parties.

The `[Internal]` ones are `IGetRemoteEncryptionKeyQCommand`, `IGetSystemListQCommand`, `ISystemListCommand`,
`IVerifyRoutedProofQCommand` and `IVerifyCallerProofCommand`. `IRevokePartyCommand` and `IReadmitPartyNameCommand` are
Critical operations of an operator.

See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-AppIdentity-Transport/blob/stable/CK.IO.AuthCenter/README.md).
